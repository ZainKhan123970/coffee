Our Story

Where craft meets
community

Milstead & Co. was born from a simple belief: that great coffee is worth obsessing over. Nestled in Seattle's vibrant Fremont neighborhood, we've been serving exceptional brews since 2012 — featuring rotating roasters and single-origin beans sourced from the world's finest farms.

Art lines our walls, water frames our windows, and every cup is made with the intention of creating something truly memorable. We don't just pour coffee — we tell a story in every sip.
